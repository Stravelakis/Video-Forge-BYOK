## Video Forge in plain English

Video Forge is a studio on your own computer for making videos where characters talk. You write the script, pick a voice for each character, and it makes the video with subtitles, in Greek or English.

- **It is not a "type a sentence, get a video" toy.** What you arrange is exactly what you get. Run it twice, get the same video.
- **You bring your own keys.** A key is a private code from an online service. The app uses your account, and the services bill you directly.
- **Everything is made on your machine.** The video is built and saved on your own computer.
- **Two voices to pick from.** One acts (you can write directions like "whispers" in the script). The other is flat but quick, handy for checking timing.
- **One script, many videos.** Make a tall, square, portrait or wide version. Or get the same video in the other language. Or fill a sheet with one row per video and let it make them all.
- **It tells viewers it was made with AI.** A small "Made with Video Forge" label is on by default.

**Good to know:** it needs a fairly strong computer (an NVIDIA graphics card with 8 GB and 32 GB of memory are recommended), and a 10-minute video takes about an hour to render. The licence is free for personal, school and paid work, with one condition: credit "Made with Video Forge" when you publish. It is source available, not classic open source.
