## Video Forge, like you're five

Imagine a little puppet theatre inside your computer.

You write what each puppet says. You pick a voice for each one. Then you press a button, and the theatre makes a real video where the puppets talk and move their mouths.

The words show up at the bottom too, so people can read along.

It does the building on your own computer, so your videos stay yours. To use some of the helpers (like voices), you paste in your own secret code, like your own library card.

It asks you to share a small note that says "Made with Video Forge" when you show your video to others. That is the one rule.
